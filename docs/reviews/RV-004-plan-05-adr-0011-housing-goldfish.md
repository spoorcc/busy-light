# Review Record RV-004

- **Subject:** Plan 05 (mechanical design, Draft) and ADR-0011 (housing concept and print process, Proposed), commit 672efab (text before this review), with `docs/requirements/mech.md` and `docs/verification/reports/mech-concept-analysis.md`
- **Type:** Design plan and architecture
- **Date:** 2026-11-04  **Reviewer(s):** five stateless goldfish subagents (two comprehension checks; three antagonistic reviews: failure modes and safety; testability, arithmetic and evidence; integration and manufacturability)  **Independent?** partly: same model family as the author
- **Not run:** the implementation-readiness pass (step 4 of the goldfish protocol). The verdict below is NO-GO on the findings, so the pass would add nothing before the actions are done.

## Comprehension check
Both comprehension goldfish restated the intent correctly: a flat 3D-printed puck (53 mm, 16.5 mm), opaque base plus translucent lid (concept B, A as fallback), antenna box of plastic only, planning only with no CAD. Their open questions are findings G1 to G6 below. No mismatch of intent, so the documents are understandable; they are not yet self-consistent.

## Outcome
**Not ready for approval. Actions required.** Verdict (implementation readiness, inferred from the findings, pass not run): **NO-GO**.
Agreed by all three antagonistic reviewers: concept B is plausible, but the plan is not self-consistent (M3, M4), the recommended configuration fails its own requirements (M1, T3), and the safety questions (T1, T2, P1) are open. I did not apply any suggestion blindly. Findings E2 (stale scores) is fixed now; the rest need either an owner decision or a design iteration.

## Actions
Owner decisions: A1 (orientation), O2 (printer and materials), the thermal limit and derating approach (T1, T2), rewording of MECH-003, MECH-005, MECH-006, and whether analysis scripts may be committed now.
Author work after the decisions: redraw joint and retention as numbered sections (M3, M4, M5, M6); re-run and commit the thermal and light scripts (T4, D1, E1); write the three-step RF test (M1); register the risks (E11); run regulatory-check (P1).
