- **Subject:** ADR-0023 `docs/decisions/ADR-0023-gate-simplification-for-one-sided-placement.md` (as of commit `b91bb05`),

## Protocol
1. Comprehension: the restatement matched the intent (decision 1: step 3 = both switches, R_ILIM3 = 56.2 k, U13 removed;
   decision 2: TPS3840 RESET and TPS2553 FAULT on one node GATE_OK_RB with R8 10 kohm to 3V3, GPIO6 on the node, U10 and R6
   removed; decision 3: smaller packages; updates 4 and 5 and the evening update). It listed 12 points it could not
   reconcile (area figures, 0402 counts, the overlap evidence behind option A, no packages-only measurement for B, the
   "input current" claim, the gate cycling, the fault-injection rows, the R_ILIM3 part, P1 or product scope, how ADR-0022
   closes, the status of the updates, missing sources). They are in the rows below.
2. Antagonistic review: two goldfish in parallel (stopped by a usage limit at 04:40 UTC and resumed at 05:25 UTC), 41 rows,
   merged below. Both recomputed the step windows with `sim/ilim_steps.py` and confirm the ADR's numbers (1368.8 to
   1553.5 mA; 54.9 k margins 13.1 and 12.7 mA) and the weighted sums (74, 49, 65, 43).
3. Resolve: the assistant checked the blocking claims in the repository: the net `/GATE_OK_RB [R8.2, U3.6, U4.6, U5.1,
   U7.4, U9.20]` (no series resistor; the EN readbacks have R14, R15); `gate.c` line 22 (`if (sel3)` = top step), line 77
   (a reset counts only when `!ctx->store.gate_flag`), line 111 (`gate_flag = true` at a gate edge), line 165 (top step for
   `advert_step >= 3`); `test_gate_logic.py` still models U13 and "HOLD_OK pulled up to the absent VBUS_IC" on the old
   `gate.net`; ADR-0018 conditions (1) and (3) (a FAULT_N test point; hold-off and fault distinguishable on the bench);
   SYS-020 (Approved) "At a constant advertised value and a constant load the device does not reset"; no file in
   `docs/decisions/` other than ADR-0023 names ADR-0023. All confirmed. Numbers marked "(reviewer)" were not re-computed.
4. Implementation readiness: not applicable (an ADR, and the hardware is built); see the outcome.

## Outcome
**Rejected** as written: decision 1 (step 3 = both switches, U13 removed) holds up, apart from the thin margin of row 6.
Decision 2 (FAULT and RESET on one node pulled up to 3V3, GPIO6 on the node) brings most of the regressions (rows 1 to 5,
13, 14) and has no alternatives of its own (row 12). The firmware has two hidden dependencies (rows 3 and 7). Before an
order the owner decides rows 1, 2, 6 and 12 (and rows 10, 14, 19, 20); the assistant then reworks the ADR (split into
separate decisions, with alternatives and sources) and the tests of rows 3, 7 and 8. The ADR's status is not changed by
this record; the owner sets it.
